# Gainly

**Know whether you are moving forward or backward today.**

Gainly is a daily money and performance tracker for people whose income changes from day to day: delivery couriers, rideshare drivers, freelancers, self-employed workers, small business owners, and anyone managing several income sources.

Instead of assuming a fixed monthly salary, Gainly helps you understand each working day, recover from negative days, and keep progressing toward a monthly income goal.

[Open the Gainly Web Preview](https://bfmix.github.io/Gainly/)

## How Gainly works

Record income and expenses in a few taps. Gainly then separates two useful views of your finances:

- **Balance** reflects your full financial position and includes every transaction.
- **Performance Balance** focuses on the work or activity you want to measure. Benefits, gifts, refunds, or other exceptional income can be excluded without disappearing from your real Balance.

Every day is classified as positive, negative, zero after activity, or without activity. This makes it easier to understand financial momentum without confusing a quiet day with a break-even day.

## What you can do

- Set optional starting balances.
- Add income and expenses through quick or detailed entry.
- Organize transactions by category, income source, payment method, date, and note.
- Create and rename your own categories and income sources.
- Choose the default Performance behavior for each category.
- Choose whether each transaction affects your Performance Balance.
- Review daily totals and recent activity on the dashboard.
- Explore performance in a monthly calendar.
- Search, filter, edit, and remove transactions.
- View statistics for a custom period.
- Set a Monthly Target and your own Daily Minimum.
- Follow the daily pace needed to reach your monthly target.
- Track streaks, positive days, and useful insights.

## Platforms and languages

Gainly is being prepared for iOS, Android, and Web. The interface supports English, French, and Spanish.

## Preview status

Gainly is under active development and should currently be treated as a testing preview. Apple sign-in, offline synchronization, notifications, linked profiles, and sharing are planned for later releases.

Do not rely on the preview as the only record of important financial information.

## Privacy

Financial information is designed to remain private to its account owner. Gainly does not currently expose profile sharing. Future sharing features will require explicit permission and will keep real Balance visibility separate from Performance information.

Gainly is a financial awareness tool. It is not an accounting, banking, tax, or investment service.
